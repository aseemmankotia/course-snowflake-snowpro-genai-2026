| 11 | Describe the two-pass exam strategy pattern. | Pass 1: answer easy/confident questions quickly, flag uncertain ones, keep moving. Pass 2: use all remaining/banked time solely on flagged questions with fresh eyes. |
| 12 | When should you switch from answering new questions to full flagged-question review mode? | At your pre-set buffer point (commonly ~10 minutes remaining), recalculated live based on your actual pace versus the ~1.3 min/question budget. |
